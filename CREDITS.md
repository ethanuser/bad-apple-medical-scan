# Source credits

**Brain MRI.** IXI – Information eXtraction from Images, EPSRC GR/S21533/02. The source project collected the scans at three London hospitals and lists its original release under CC BY-SA 3.0. [IXI dataset](https://brain-development.org/ixi-dataset/)

**Images and brain masks used here.** Andrew Hoopes, Jocelyn S. Mora, Adrian V. Dalca, Bruce Fischl and Malte Hoffmann, *SynthStrip: Skull-Stripping for Any Brain Image*, NeuroImage 260, 119474 (2022), dataset v1.5. This release supplies original native-space images and manually refined masks. Its terms specify CC BY-SA 4.0 for IXI images and CC BY 4.0 for other content. The pinned files were served by the MedOtter mirror. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2022.119474); [Release, terms and authors](https://surfer.nmr.mgh.harvard.edu/docs/synthstrip/data/README); [Mirror](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MedOtter/SynthStrip/tree/d5a92c65c549b2a099bdb5a6e61ebeed6cb6eca3)

**Whole-body CT.** Jakob Wasserthal, *Dataset with segmentations of 117 important anatomical structures in 1228 CT images*, version 2.0.1, Zenodo (2023), CC BY 4.0. Described in Wasserthal et al., *TotalSegmentator: Robust Segmentation of 104 Anatomic Structures in CT Images*, Radiology: Artificial Intelligence (2023). [Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/10047292)

**Adapted imagery.** The MRI and CT crops, thumbnails and slice panels in this project are available under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International. Changes comprise brain masking; for CT, removal of the head above the clavicles, body masking and a fixed −1000 to +1000 HU window; intensity normalization, orientation of voxel axes, cropping, uniform physical scaling, in-plane rotation, optional mirroring or intensity inversion, smoothing by 0.6 voxels and thresholding to black and white. The LOCAL overlay marks the crop footprint. [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

**Animation and sound.** *Bad Apple!!* shadow PV by Anira. Music by Alstroemeria Records, featuring nomico, based on the composition by ZUN / Team Shanghai Alice. The local proof uses an excerpt from a pinned public encode. The animation and soundtrack retain their creators’ rights. Public uploads require an appropriate basis for using those works alongside the CC-licensed MRI material. [Shadow PV](https://www.nicovideo.jp/watch/sm8628149); [Pinned encode](https://github.com/bad-apple-lab/Bad-Apple/tree/80e39ed58d754e4de420b7a1c474e53e20a71ba3)

**Visual reference.** The large match, small original, scan grid, local crop and running score follow the layout of ikuto32’s lunar edition. The MRI search and Python renderer were written for this project. [Bad Apple!! on real Earth and the real Moon](https://github.com/ikuto32/bad-apple-earth-moon)
